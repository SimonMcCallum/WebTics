Quickstart

1. Claim your account

Your instructor emails you an email and a temporary password. Go to Claim your account, set your own password, and choose your display name. Your access is active for the length of your course.

2. Register your game

On your dashboard, register a game. You'll get a measurement_id (public) and an api_secret (shown once — keep it safe). This is the same idea as a Google Analytics property + API secret.

3a. Godot (recommended for class)

Copy the ludogogy_logging addon into res://addons/, enable it in Project → Project Settings → Plugins, then:

func _ready():
    LudogogyLogging.start("WT-XXXXXXXX", "your-api-secret")
    LudogogyLogging.event("level_start", { "level": 1 })

func _on_level_complete(score):
    LudogogyLogging.event("level_up", { "level": 2 })
    LudogogyLogging.event("post_score", { "score": score })

3b. Web games

<script src="{{ public_base_url }}/sdk/webtics.js"></script>
<script>
  webtics('config', 'WT-XXXXXXXX', { api_secret: 'your-api-secret' });
  webtics('event', 'level_up', { level: 5, character: 'mage' });
</script>

Event names

Use GA4/Apple-style names where they fit (session_start, level_up, post_score, unlock_achievement, purchase, …) so your skills transfer to the paid tools. Any other name works too — it's stored as a custom event. See the full list at /mp/event-registry.

Limits

Each game has a rate limit (events per minute) and a total storage cap. If you hit the cap, new events are rejected (HTTP 429) but your existing data is kept safe. Watch your usage bars on the dashboard; email your instructor if you need more.
